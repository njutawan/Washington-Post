Two paintings by the French Impressionist master Pierre-Auguste Renoir have
disappeared from a regional museum in southern France, in what prosecutors
described Monday as a carefully planned overnight heist.

## What we know

Museum officials discovered the works — "Woman with a Parasol" and an 1875 study
for "Dance at Le Moulin de la Galette" — missing from their frames early Saturday.
Surveillance footage shows at least two individuals entering through a service
entrance shortly after 2 a.m.

French Interior Minister called the theft "an attack on our national heritage."
