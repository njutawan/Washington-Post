## Behind the lens

For two days our photographers moved between the Capitol, the White House and K Street, carrying two bodies each and working past midnight. The photograph of the Speaker alone in the Rayburn Room, above, came at the end of a long corridor where no reporter was permitted — it was taken through a cracked door.

## How the vote came together

The final vote was 312 to 102 — a bipartisan majority that surprised even some senior aides. In the end, 88 Republicans crossed over to vote with Democrats.

The next deadline is now Nov. 15, when the stopgap expires and negotiators will have to reach agreement on a full-year spending bill.
