Compact discs are outselling vinyl records for the first time since 2001,
according to new retail data — and nobody is more surprised than the people
who spent the 2010s insisting the format was dead forever.

## Why the comeback?

Industry observers point to three factors: K-pop superfans buying deluxe boxed
sets, a nostalgia wave among Gen Z listeners who missed CD booklets the first
time around, and a growing frustration with streaming-service payouts.

"My first car had a CD player," said one 22-year-old fan outside a record store
in Silver Spring. "So I bought a 100-pack of blanks and burned mixes for the
drive to school."
